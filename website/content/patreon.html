Unlocking, step by step

  1. Install the toolkit as usual. These tools need no special installer; they are rows in the same catalogue. The three steps are here.
  2. Open FNS_Updater inside the toolkit container. Its sign-in parameters are where all of this happens, and there is no separate account tool to install.
  3. Pulse Sign In, or paste a Gumroad licence key and pulse Redeem. Signing in opens Patreon in your normal browser, you approve there, and the tab hands the result back to TouchDesigner by itself.
  4. Pick your tools. The tools your tier or key covers stop being locked in the picker and install like anything else.

What it does to your machine

Worth being precise about, because the answer is short and it is the part people are right to ask about.

  • The credential never touches your project. It goes into the Windows Credential Manager or the macOS Keychain, under your user account. It stays out of the .toe, out of any .tox, and out of files beside them, so a project you hand to a collaborator carries no key of yours.
  • TouchDesigner never holds a Patreon token. It holds an opaque token that means nothing anywhere else, and that can be revoked.
  • The check is on the bytes, not the buttons. Entitlement is decided by the server before a file is served. Nothing is "unlocked" by editing a parameter, which is also why a locked tool in your picker is a real lock.
  • Sign out whenever. One button; the credential is deleted from the keystore.

The questions people actually ask

What happens if my membership lapses?

Tools already installed in your project keep working. They are files in your .toe like any other component; nothing calls home to check on them, and nothing expires.

What you lose is the ability to download them again: updates to those tools, and installing them on a new machine or into a new project. Rejoin and both come back.

Does any of this change the free toolkit?

No. The free packages are served publicly and unauthenticated exactly as they were, the source stays on GitHub under MIT, and the installer does not ask you to sign in to use them. If you never unlock anything, you will not notice the gate exists beyond a few cards marked Patreon.

Nothing that is free today becomes paid later. The Patreon list grows by new tools arriving in it.

Can I still read the source of a Patreon tool?

These packages are not published to the public GitHub repository, and they are not MIT. Once installed they are ordinary TouchDesigner components in your project; you can open them, read the Python inside, and see exactly what they do. What you may not do is redistribute them.

I work on several machines: studio, laptop, a show box.

Connect each one. A membership is checked per machine against your account, so signing in on a new box is the whole procedure.

Gumroad licence keys are per tool, and how many machines one key covers is stated on the Gumroad page.

Does the toolkit need internet to run?

Only to install and to update. Once packages are in your project, TouchDesigner runs them offline, which matters on a show machine, and is why the entitlement check sits on the download, with nothing checked at startup.

Why gate anything at all?

Patreon memberships and Gumroad purchases help pay for development and maintenance of FNSTools, including the free tools.
